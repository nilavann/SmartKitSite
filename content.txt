ABOUT US

Where Learning Meets Confidence and Creativity

Smart Kids Skill Development Centre was founded in August 2015 by R. Saranyadevi, an educator, entrepreneur, and professional skill development trainer with over 12 years of experience in education and training.

Our centre was established with a simple vision: to provide children with a supportive learning environment where academic excellence meets practical life skills.

We focus not only on improving marks but also on developing communication, creativity, concentration, problem-solving abilities, discipline, and self-confidence.

Our Vision

To inspire every child to become a confident, capable, creative, and lifelong learner.

Our Mission

To deliver engaging, affordable, and personalised learning experiences that strengthen academic knowledge, develop essential skills, and encourage every child's individual potential.

OUR LEARNING APPROACH

A Strong Foundation for a Successful Future

At Smart Kids, we follow a child-centred learning approach based on five essential principles:

1.⁠ ⁠Understand – Build strong conceptual knowledge.
2.⁠ ⁠Practise – Develop skills through regular activities.
3.⁠ ⁠Explore – Encourage curiosity and creativity.
4.⁠ ⁠Express – Improve communication and confidence.
5.⁠ ⁠Achieve – Celebrate individual progress and accomplishments.

We believe that consistent encouragement, effective teaching, and a positive environment help children discover their true potential.
