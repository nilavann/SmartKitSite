Questions parents ask

Timings, batch size, fees, progress updates
What age can my child join?

Our programmes are for children aged 4 and above.

Is Smart Kids a tuition centre?

Smart Kids is a skill development and tuition centre in Tiruchengode. Along with teaching school subjects, exam preparation and Olympiad practice, we run courses in abacus, Vedic maths, phonics, spoken English, Hindi, Tamil, handwriting, art and life skills.

Which courses do you offer?

We offer 40 courses in six programmes: Academic Excellence, Language and Communication, Brain Development and Mental Maths, Handwriting and Creative Writing, Art, Creativity and Activities, and Personality and Life Skills.

Do you offer online classes?

Yes. Classes run online, and offline at our centres in Tiruchengode.

Do you run summer camps?

Yes. Our summer camps and holiday learning clubs run Monday to Saturday, 9:30 AM to 12:30 PM, and combine academics, creativity, physical activity and play.

When do classes run?

We run after-school and weekend batches. Call us and we will suggest a batch that fits your child's school timetable.

How many children are in a batch?

Batches are kept small so every child gets individual attention. Ask us about the batch for your child's course.

How much are the fees?

Fees depend on the course and how often your child attends. Send an enquiry and we will share the details for the course you choose.

Do you offer one-to-one support?

Yes, individual learning support is available.

How will I know my child is improving?

Ask us how we share updates with parents for your child's course.
